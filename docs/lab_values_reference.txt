# Medical Reference: Common Lab Values
# Source: Adapted from Clinical Laboratory Reference Ranges

## Complete Blood Count (CBC)

White Blood Cell Count (WBC):
- Normal: 4,500-11,000 cells/uL
- Low (Leukopenia): Below 4,500 - Increased infection risk
- High (Leukocytosis): Above 11,000 - May indicate infection or inflammation
- Critical Low: Below 2,000 - Severe immunocompromise
- Critical High: Above 30,000 - May indicate leukemia or severe infection

Hemoglobin:
- Normal Male: 13.5-17.5 g/dL
- Normal Female: 12.0-16.0 g/dL
- Low (Anemia): Below normal range - Fatigue, weakness
- Critical Low: Below 7.0 g/dL - May require transfusion
- High: Above normal - May indicate dehydration or polycythemia

## Kidney Function Tests

Creatinine:
- Normal Male: 0.7-1.3 mg/dL
- Normal Female: 0.6-1.1 mg/dL
- Elevated: Above normal - May indicate kidney dysfunction
- High: Above 2.0 mg/dL - Significant kidney impairment
- Critical: Above 4.0 mg/dL - Severe kidney failure, may need dialysis

Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN):
- Normal: 7-20 mg/dL
- Elevated: Above 20 mg/dL - Kidney disease, dehydration, or high protein diet
- Critical: Above 50 mg/dL - Severe kidney dysfunction

## Lipid Panel

Total Cholesterol:
- Desirable: Less than 200 mg/dL
- Borderline High: 200-239 mg/dL
- High: 240 mg/dL or above

LDL Cholesterol (Bad):
- Optimal: Less than 100 mg/dL
- Near Optimal: 100-129 mg/dL
- Borderline High: 130-159 mg/dL
- High: 160-189 mg/dL
- Very High: 190 mg/dL or above

HDL Cholesterol (Good):
- Low (Risk Factor): Less than 40 mg/dL
- Normal: 40-59 mg/dL
- High (Protective): 60 mg/dL or above

Triglycerides:
- Normal: Less than 150 mg/dL
- Borderline High: 150-199 mg/dL
- High: 200-499 mg/dL
- Very High: 500 mg/dL or above - Risk of pancreatitis

## Liver Function Tests

ALT (Alanine Aminotransferase):
- Normal: 7-56 U/L
- Elevated: Above 56 U/L - May indicate liver inflammation
- High: Above 200 U/L - Significant liver damage

AST (Aspartate Aminotransferase):
- Normal: 10-40 U/L
- Elevated: Above 40 U/L - Liver or heart muscle damage

## Electrolytes

Sodium:
- Normal: 136-145 mEq/L
- Low (Hyponatremia): Below 136 - Confusion, seizures risk
- Critical Low: Below 120 - Medical emergency
- High (Hypernatremia): Above 145 - Dehydration

Potassium:
- Normal: 3.5-5.0 mEq/L
- Low (Hypokalemia): Below 3.5 - Weakness, cardiac arrhythmias
- Critical Low: Below 2.5 - Life-threatening arrhythmias
- High (Hyperkalemia): Above 5.0 - Cardiac risk
- Critical High: Above 6.5 - Medical emergency, cardiac arrest risk
